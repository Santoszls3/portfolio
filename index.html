SANTOS

GONZALES

Santos Gonzales is a web developer by day, and a graphic design enthusiast by night. Passionate about all things code, he's on a relentless quest to refine his skills, because as he says, “If you're not debugging, are you even coding?” While web development has captured his full attention, Santos also finds time to explore his other loves: dogs, gardening, and pretending he knows how to mountain bike (spoiler: the dog is more reliable on trails). When he's not immersed in code or on a hike, you'll probably find him crafting the perfect garden bed, because even his plants deserve top tier design.
